Stay Safe Online

Always follow these basic security practices.

✅ Verify the website URL before entering personal information.
✅ Avoid clicking suspicious links received through unknown messages or emails.
✅ Never share passwords or sensitive information with untrusted websites.
✅ Use strong and unique passwords for your accounts.
